Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our courses

What is the duration of courses?

Our courses range from 1 to 6 months depending on the program. Professional Makeup is 3 months, Hair Styling is 4 months, and Skincare is 2 months. We also offer fast-track options for experienced students.

Do you provide certification?

Yes! All our courses come with internationally recognized certification upon completion. Our certificates are accepted worldwide and help you start your career with credibility.

What is the class schedule?

We offer flexible schedules: morning (9AM-1PM), afternoon (2PM-6PM), and weekend batches (Saturday & Sunday). Choose what works best for you!

Do you offer job placement?

Yes! We have partnerships with over 50 salons and spas. Our placement cell provides 100% assistance in job placements, internships, and freelancing opportunities.

What materials are included?

Course fees include study materials, professional kit (makeup brushes, tools, etc.), and access to our practice lab. Some advanced products may be extra.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans. You can pay in 2-3 installments. Contact our admission office for details.
