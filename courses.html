Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our courses

What is the duration of courses?

Our courses range from 1 to 6 months depending on the program. Professional Makeup is 3 months, Hair Styling is 4 months, and Skincare is 2 months.

Do you provide certification?

Yes! All our courses come with internationally recognized certification upon completion. Our certificates are accepted worldwide.

What is the class schedule?

We offer flexible schedules: morning (9AM-1PM), afternoon (2PM-6PM), and weekend batches (Saturday & Sunday).

Do you offer job placement?

Yes! We have partnerships with over 50 salons and spas. Our placement cell provides 100% assistance in job placements.

What materials are included?

Course fees include study materials, professional kit, and access to our practice lab. Some advanced products may be extra.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans. You can pay in 2-3 installments. Contact our admission office for details.
